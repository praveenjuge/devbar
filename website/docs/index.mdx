## Up and running in a minute

<Steps titleSize="h4">
  <Step title="Download" icon="download">
    Get the `.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/praveenjuge/devbar/releases/latest).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it" icon="app-window">
    Drag Devbar to Applications and open it. It's signed and notarized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the gauge" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    Find the icon in your menu bar. Your quota and dev servers are already there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Questions

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="Do I need to sign in to anything?">
    No. Devbar reuses the logins your Codex CLI, Claude Code, and Muse already saved, and only reads them.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Does it work on Intel Macs?">
    Not yet. Releases are built for Apple Silicon and need macOS 14 or later.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Can deleting worktrees lose my work?">
    Devbar always asks first, and it warns you when a worktree has uncommitted changes or is locked by an agent.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Does it collect any data?">
    No. There's no account and no analytics. [See exactly what it reads](/privacy).
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
